# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing Sellbird no-build web (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS) is the established frontend; ONE ships as a no-build static app beside it so it deploys as a static site. Source of truth for scope: docs/gtm/ONE-BRIEF.md.

## Users
The whole GTM organization on one shared intelligence layer: founder, head of GTM, marketing, sales manager, SDR/BDR, AE, event manager, RevOps, product. Primary demo user: a founder running sales at a ~28-person B2B SaaS company, checking daily what matters and what to do next. A role switcher changes recommendations.

## Product Purpose
ONE is an AI-native GTM operating system: it connects the existing stack (CRM, enrichment, email, meetings, events, ads, product analytics), finds what is happening and why, recommends actions with evidence, executes with approval, and learns from outcomes. Success: a visitor understands the purpose within 30 seconds and sees strategy improve over time.

## Positioning
ONE learns: ICPs and strategies are evolving hypotheses revised by outcomes (meetings, lost deals, events, competitor moves), every recommendation exposes its evidence, confidence and limitations, and agents disagree in the open before a human approves. Not a CRM, lead tool, outreach automation or dashboard collection.

## Constraints
- All demo data is synthetic (fictional customer TraceLayer, AI agent observability, 90-day period) and labeled as such; simulated integrations are marked simulated.
- High-impact actions (sends, budget changes) require approval.
- No invented competitor revenue or private metrics; forecasts are never guarantees.
- No buttons that look functional but do nothing.
- Design references (brief): Linear's simplicity, Attio's density, neutral palette, restrained accent; avoid gradients, glass, decorative AI graphics, generic dashboard templates.
